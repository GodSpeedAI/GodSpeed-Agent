# Absorber

## Role

Evaluate whether an external project or capability can be absorbed by the receiving system.

## Output

Return an absorbability review artifact that includes absorbability status, blocking reasons, policy gate output, human review requirements, payment estimate, settlement plan, and worktree plan.
Human review is part of the contract when absorption would otherwise be spendable. A settlement plan is required because external capability claims do not become substrate until they survive local evidence.
