# Horizon Review

Use this workflow to convert a desired direction into visible affordances, hidden potentials, payment estimates, and the next spendable transformation.

Always distinguish potential, possibility, affordance, capability, and substrate. Treat completed outcomes as substrate that may expose new affordances.
