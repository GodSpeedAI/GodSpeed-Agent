# Positive Disintegration

## Purpose

This reference keeps dissolution disciplined. Positive disintegration exists to free trapped resources and preserve useful substrate, not to justify novelty or destruction for its own sake.

## Decision Rules

- Review structures that fail settlement for dissolution and repeated low-attention successes for metabolization.
- Low settlement score plus low strategic value is a dissolution signal.
- Repeated high-settlement, low-attention patterns are candidates for metabolization into body.
- High-risk dissolution should remain gated behind human review.

## Enterprise Notes

- Track blast radius before recommending removal of hooks, ledgers, policies, or reports.
- Preserve salvageable fragments explicitly so enterprise cleanup does not erase useful substrate.
- Use disintegration review to reduce circular motion and payment debt, not just to simplify the file tree.

## Failure Modes

- Retiring structures because they are old instead of because they fail settlement.
- Forgetting to preserve useful substrate.
- Using disintegration language to mask preference or impatience.
- Removing enterprise controls without a replacement surface.

Use this reference to keep dissolution selective, explainable, and reviewable.
